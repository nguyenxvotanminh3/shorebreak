VUC LANG — underwater surface and color candidate

Reference observations
The first supplied reference is an above-water illustration with dark blue-gray
wave faces, broken silver highlights and surface relief. The second directly
shows the desired underwater view: a bright cyan light window overhead,
irregular dark wave streaks, blue distant water and readable nearby structures.
Only water, light and color cues are used; no ship, base, UI or image texture
from either reference is included in the game.

Implementation
- One viewer-following 720m surface patch, 8,192 triangles and one draw
- Low-frequency vertex swell plus wind-aligned irregular analytic normal bands
- 3 / 4 / 5 bounded normal octaves for Low / Medium / High
- Derivative-based detail fading reduces fine-distance shimmer
- View-angle water/air Fresnel and critical-window cues, distorted by wave normals
- Procedural sky radiance with broad cloud/silver highlight variation
- Blue-gray reflected-light approximation outside the transmitted light window
- Shared wavelength-dependent eye-path absorption/scattering for the surface
  and world materials, with red attenuating faster than green/blue
- Neutral blue-white nearby ambient/sunlight instead of the earlier green cast
- Fog fallback for actors, distant background and optical haze share a color

Budget and scope
No offscreen capture, reflection/refraction render target, extra render pass,
texture download, per-frame material/geometry creation or additional lights.
The original world interface, terrain, gameplay colliders and arm code remain
unchanged. Quality changes reuse uniforms without new shader variants.

This is art-directed single-pass water, not physically exact ocean transport.
The light window refracts a procedural sky, not actual above-water scene
objects. Reflected underwater radiance is analytical, not a captured scene.
It does not add fluid simulation, real volumetric scattering or a ray tracer.

Verification
Source suite: 87 tests pass, including 8 new optical/resource checks for
transmission/Fresnel bounds, r170 shader chunks and ordering, no double fog,
finite patch coverage, tier work limits, 1,200 stable updates and disposal.
These checks do not compile GPU GLSL or validate new rendered appearance.
The Mac overlay has been handed off for real shallow/upward, grazing/horizon,

Earlier Mac performance numbers describe previous candidates. They are not
benchmarks of this shader. No new push, merge or deployment is included.
