VỰC LẶNG — Blender habitat and tissue integration candidate

The art pass follows the articulated-motion milestone, whose actual Mac results
are documented in MOTION-MAC-QA.md. The older motion commit and review ZIP remain
preserved. This candidate is a separate local branch; it is not published.

BLENDER VEGETATION
One1,586,968-byte GLB contains six grounded species with paired full/low meshes:
ribbon kelp1220/132 triangles, split kelp1042/144, eelgrass916/132, red algae1120/152,
sea fan1192/188, and plate coral1080/108. These are authored textured meshes, not
billboard image substitutes. The fan's lattice has actual openings. All share
one opaque material and1024px color plus512px normal/ORM maps.

The game reuses its seeded habitat rather than changing terrain, objectives or
colliders. COLOR_0 alpha becomes a dedicated root-to-tip bend attribute; it never
makes the atlas transparent. Shared GPU sway keeps roots planted and delays the
tips. Camera-near detailed plants and lower-detail plants use pooled instancing,
with global high/medium/low limits of320/220/140 total and96/64/0 full meshes.
Maximum plant draws are12/12/6, before view-dependent visibility. Old procedural
kelp/fans remain as a load-error fallback and are hidden after assets are ready.

KRAKEN REFINEMENT
The new full/low exports are53,901/18,000 triangles and3,768,704/1,441,556 bytes.
Organic mantle folds, finer surface texture, recessed eyes and locally weighted
suckers were improved. All59 bone names, bind transforms and animation samples
match the preceding production rig exactly. The stronger runtime extension and
recurl controls remain unchanged. The modeler's Blender renders include those
extreme poses; those renders are separate from browser QA of this new art.

JELLY TISSUE
One shared261,387-byte512px RGBA DATA atlas adds canals, internal tissue rosettes,
pleats and thickness variation. It is sampled within the existing two materials;
no extra mesh, vertex, render pass or draw is added. The game retains the current
deforming procedural shell and length-preserving trails. The Blender source-art
mesh/morph study is not a runtime replacement or proof of the latest motion.

VALIDATION AND LIMITS
Actual asset bytes, hashes, geometry, rig and loading/lifecycle contracts are
verified in automated checks. Blender previews of the final art were inspected.
The Mac disconnected after the preceding motion review, so this integrated art
has not yet been accepted in actual game pixels, shader compilation or frame
measurements. Previous FPS/rAF samples must not be presented as measurements of
this candidate. Retest near plants, depth/fog, transparent tissue, quality changes,
LOD transitions, continuous creature cycles and real gameplay when available.
