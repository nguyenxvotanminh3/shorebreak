SHOREBREAK — research and implementation notes

Design: arcade surfing driven by slope, speed and rhythm. A lateral pocket coordinate follows the travelling swell so the player can focus on carving rather than paddling. This is an accessible surfing game, not a full fluid or rigid-body simulation.

Surf mechanics
- Descending a wave converts gravitational potential into speed; climbing consumes speed. Carving connects these phases. https://annex.exploratorium.edu/the-world/surfing/physics/surfsci2.html
- Energy-rich region near the curl motivates a soft pocket speed/scoring reward. https://scripps.ucsd.edu/news/waves-sweet-spot-revealed
- Applied: slope-sensitive acceleration; downhill pumping only; smoothed lean; alternating carve rewards; charge/release aerial near crest; clean versus unbalanced landing; temporary wipeout recovery.

Water and performance
- Geometric waves and fine normal detail separate large-scale shape from small-scale reflection. https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-1-effective-water-simulation-physical-models
- Fresnel water reflectance with F0=0.02 and exponent 5, informed by Three.js Water. https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/jsm/objects/Water.js
- Applied: shaped travelling heightfield with directional wind waves; identical analytic CPU/GPU surface and derivatives; procedural sky reflection, sun highlights, crest foam and board wake. Heightfield does not create an overhanging barrel or physical underwater refraction.
- Resolution constrained by pixel budget; mobile starts low; quality adapts after sustained frame-time evidence. https://threejs.org/manual/en/responsive.html
- One animation loop, fixed 1/60-second physics, pooled particles, no per-frame texture or geometry allocation, HUD at 10 Hz, pause on hidden tab. https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/requestAnimationFrame
- Low draw counts and no synchronous GPU readback in game loop. https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL_API/WebGL_best_practices

Quality budgets
Low: 90x90 grid, 0.75 megapixels, DPR <= 1.
Balanced: 130x130 grid, 1.4 megapixels, DPR <= 1.3.
High: 180x180 grid, 2.3 megapixels, DPR <= 1.6.
Gameplay uses the same fixed timestep and water formula at all levels.

Dependencies
Three.js 0.170.0 (MIT), vendored locally with license; browser loads no remote JavaScript. Optional Google Fonts have system fallbacks.

Validation
Desktop Chromium: scene renders; ~60 FPS observed in this environment, 33-34 draw calls; carving, charged aerial, clean landing, score/combo, pause freezing time verified by actual session state.
These figures are observations on the test machine, not guarantees for all devices.
Additional validation: mobile viewport 393x852 has no horizontal overflow; on-screen steering changes the pocket offset; low tier renders at 393x852 instead of device DPR 3. Numerical checks at 20 points give maximum analytic gradient error 5.05e-9. Non-lip hop grants no aerial combo; lip launch and clean landing grant one. Out-of-pocket wipeout and automatic recovery verified. WebMCP registry is feature-detected, but this test browser does not support document.modelContext, so tool registration could not be verified there.
A complete 90-second run reached the result dialog at simulation time 90.0167 seconds, persisted the best score, and returned to the menu. Free surf starts with an infinite timer. Verification used desktop Chromium and mobile emulation, not a physical phone.

VERSION 2 — athlete, professional motion and sea encounters

A real anatomical, continuous MakeHuman-derived skinned human replaces the assembled primitive body. Asset: 52 joints, 28,816 triangles, photo skin on head/hands/feet, authored wetsuit, ~1.12MB, all textures embedded. CC0 provenance is in dist/assets/PROVENANCE.txt; no source motion-capture animations are shipped.

Motion references inspected:
https://www.surfer.com/how-to/frontside-air-reverse-1
https://www.redbull.com/int-en/red-bull-airborne-2019-highlights
https://www.redbull.com/us-en/videos/sessions-lower-trestles-griffin-colapinto-down-the-line
https://surfhappens.com/how-to-surf/videos/how-to-do-a-frontside-air-reverse-with-lakey-peterson/
https://ojs.ub.uni-konstanz.de/cpa/article/view/6883/6179

Authored motion follows compression, extension, asymmetric knee tuck/rail grab, spotting the landing, knee absorption and recovery. Runtime two-bone IK keeps the anatomical feet on the board while bending the continuous mesh at joints. This is procedural animation informed by references, not captured motion from those athletes.

Physics uses fixed 120Hz steps with render interpolation. Lateral rail grip has momentum; airborne X/Z velocity is preserved independently of wave motion. Surface orientation samples nose/tail/rails. Lip takeoff speed is tuned for readable gameplay (roughly 3–5m height), not claimed as a measured professional-athlete average. Test launch reached 4.58m with ~1.92s airtime, landed cleanly; 360 launch/landing also verified. Identical control schedules at render rates 30, 60 and 144Hz produced matching positions and speed.

Four original animated sea obstacles: shark, kraken, sea serpent and jellyfish. Encounter warnings appear before contact; jump clearance accounts for board height; collisions reduce speed/reset combo and grant brief recovery immunity. Verified shark collision and a successful jump over the shark in deterministic simulation.

VERSION 4.2 — held aura, animation stability and organic creature surfaces

Input and animation fixes
- E now has immediate held intent (auraHeld), including a grounded salute and visible open-arc FX. Scoring remains gated by the original safe mid-air window (auraActive); grounded posing earns no aura bonus.
- Key sources and touch pointer IDs are tracked independently. Releasing one alias/pointer cannot cancel another held source. Pause, blur, restart and dialog transitions clear intent; repeat events cannot restore stale held inputs.
- Zero delta means frozen animation. Pause/finish skip visual easing, camera and wave updates; audio is updated separately so pausing also mutes wind. A hard airborne gravity-blend cutoff was replaced by exponential damping.
- Three air poses blend through time instead of snapping between styles. IK deck contacts remain fixed; actual GLB bone tests check the foot positions and visible hand displacement.

Graphics implementation
- Creature skin uses deterministic shared 128x128 albedo, roughness and tangent-space normal maps. Broad pigmentation is baked in bind space; UVs stay attached during deformation. Skin is nonmetallic, with restrained glow and more anatomical kaiju/kraken forms.
- One directional light casts PCFSoft self/other-object shadows near the rider: 1024 balanced, 2048 high, disabled low. A cooler hemisphere/rim fill preserves volume rather than washing the forms out. The shadow region follows the endless course.
- A separate reusable wave-conforming contact-shade mesh grounds the rider and nearby obstacles at every tier. It is an artistic contact approximation, not a physically exact reflected or refracted shadow.
- Reference: Three.js r170 shadow guidance, including the cost of additional casting lights and the alternative of contact planes: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrdoob/three.js/r170/manual/en/shadows.html
- Frame timing reference: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Window/requestAnimationFrame

Validation boundary for 4.2
- node --test tests/*.test.mjs runs dependency-free physics/IK, animation, input, lighting and creature-buffer checks, plus the production application handlers/frame loop with Node DOM and renderer stand-ins.
- The stand-ins test logic, pause/resume, key events, touch cancellation and quality transitions. They DO NOT validate GPU shaders, real browser keyboard input, screenshots, draw-call timing or actual device FPS.
- Local HTTP server health returned 200, but the cloud browser could not reach the preview service in this environment. No before/after pixel comparison was completed. Visual and GPU review remains required before release; previous-version browser observations above are not claims about 4.2.
