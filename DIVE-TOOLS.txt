VỰC LẶNG — steering, sonar and flashlight revision
Build label: dive-tools-v5 (visible in Settings and the read-only debug snapshot)

Movement diagnosis and correction
The camera's YXZ forward/right basis was correct across 7,920 heading/input
combinations. A reproducible usability defect was world-space acceleration
lag while holding W and turning: a sustained right turn produced 1.563 m/s
camera-left velocity. Input-produced momentum now follows active viewing
changes. Contact deflections and creature impulses remain in world space;
changed movement controls, neutral coasting, gravity and release braking keep
their prior behavior. The regression case is now at numerical zero lateral
speed. This does not establish whether the reported browser also had contact,
stale-version or held-input issues. The build label and lateral/resolved speed
instrumentation help distinguish them. Player input still clears on blur,
pause, cancellation and reset; full rest remains exact.

Sonar
Q emits one bounded 140 m scan with a nine-second cooldown. An echo only arrives
after its outbound/return distance delay. It reports distance, bearing and depth,
and fades five seconds after arrival. The HUD shows up to three useful nearby
returns; world markers use arrived echoes rather than a global timer revealing
arbitrarily distant targets. Nearby directly visible landmarks and the known
return buoy retain separate passive navigation cues. Objectives still require
physical travel and holding E; sonar neither collects nor saves samples.

The contact set includes uncollected samples, the buoy, diving-bell air pocket,
rig entrance and three large creatures. Creature returns are saved emission
positions, labeled as old observations, not live tracking. The Warden contact
uses an approximate body center 12 m above its grounded root. The sensor can
hold at most16 returns from at most256 supplied contacts, independent of visual
quality. It pauses with the expedition, resets on new dive, and is rejected or
cleared when the receiver is in air/a dry room.

Real nominal sound speed1500 m/s gives a140 m round trip in about0.187s.
The display expands this to2.8s for readable feedback and labels the expansion.
This is a game sensor, omitting acoustic obstruction, multipath, refraction,
noise and Doppler. It is not a physical sonar simulator.
References: https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/technology/sonar/
https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/sound.html

Flashlight
F toggles one camera-centered real spotlight. Its target follows the camera's
world-space forward axis. Inverse-square falloff, soft cone and one shadow map
make the beam distance-dependent and allow terrain/opaque walls/doors to block
it. No decorative cone mesh or screen-brightness overlay is used. Dynamic
skinned creatures can receive shadows but do not cast them in this budget;
shader-swaying vegetation is also excluded from casting without a matching
custom depth shader. Transparent objects do not cast opaque wall shadows.

Low/Medium/High: range30/38/46m; shadow map256/512/768px; maximum scheduled
refresh12/20/30Hz. Unchanged stationary shadows are reused. Moving doors dirty
the map. Shadow caching can lag motion by up to83/50/33ms. This adds a bounded
shadow rendering pass and must be profiled on hardware; no FPS claim is made.

Deep ambient/sun/fill endpoints change from .48/.24/.24 to .14/.055/.045.
Shallow daylight stays2/2.5/.65. Explicit interior factors are .5/.08/.08;
local rig lamps remain. The torch is centered near the eye instead of behind
the right palm, and a verified r170 arm-material hook caps only its incident
illumination before BRDF evaluation. Other lights, textures and ordinary depth
occlusion remain. Hook failure cleans up the partial owned arm model safely.
