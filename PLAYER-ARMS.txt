VỰC LẶNG — camera-mounted diver arms

Purpose
Two visible anatomical gloved hands and wetsuit forearms replace the empty
first-person foreground. This change prioritizes player presence; creature
behavior is unchanged.

Animation
Four Blender-authored skeletal clips: idle, swim, sprint and scan. Swimming
strength and tempo follow actual simulation velocity, including inertial
slowdown, rather than starting a rigid looping prop whenever W is pressed.
Sprint blends in only as the diver gains speed. Held E near a valid signal
blends into the deliberate right-hand interaction pose. Releasing or losing
the target smoothly returns to the appropriate movement state.
Pause freezes the mixer, transitions and sway. New dives reset the rig.
Reduced-motion mode removes idle drift and look sway while preserving the
functional swimming and interaction actions.

Perspective and occlusion
The real rig is parented to the existing perspective camera in the same
world scene, with depthTest/depthWrite enabled. There is no always-on-top
foreground overlay and no extra render pass. World geometry can occlude hands.
Short hand-space probes use the real seafloor and cylindrical solid colliders
to gently retract the rig at close surfaces. This is not full contact IK.
Narrow screens shift the rig forward to retain hand framing without stretching
or compressing the anatomy horizontally. Clip framing and near-plane checks
are recorded in the asset's validation/metadata.

Implementation
abyss-arms.js owns the one-time GLB load, pooled animation actions, pose blending,
short collision probes, reset and complete resource disposal. abyss.js connects
it to actual speed, sprint/scan intent, pause/menu status, reduced motion and
camera aspect. Read-only debug snapshots expose the arm load/pose state for QA.

Asset provenance
The anatomical hands/forearms are adapted from this repository's licensed
MakeHuman CC0 athlete. The dedicated view rig, wetsuit/glove surface treatment,
framing and four clips were authored for this game in Blender. Existing CC0
notices are preserved; detailed provenance accompanies the GLB.

Verification status
Full source suite: 79 tests pass, including 17 arms/controller/real-GLB/portrait
checks. Separate actual Mac WebGL2 inspection observed idle/swim/sprint/scan,
release smoothing, pause/home/reset, blocked movement and deep-water hands.
Portrait clipping was fixed and screenshot-verified at 390x844 and 320x844.
The portrait correction moves the rig farther away without stretching anatomy;
it withdraws the extra reach near walls/floor. Narrower aspects are not promised.

Read ARMS-MAC-QA.md for exact coverage. Looking nearly straight down while
resting on the floor can still hide the hands and expose a forearm edge; this
is not full contact IK. The right-mounted torch brightens the right hand much
more than the left. No unverified contact/lighting change was made after QA.
Actual arms-build Medium rAF samples were ~57.9 FPS deep and ~54 FPS at the
shallow reef, seven seconds each in different scenes, not an A/B GPU benchmark.
The older numbers in MAC-QA.md predate the arms and remain separate.
This arm feature is local only; no push, merge or deployment is included.
