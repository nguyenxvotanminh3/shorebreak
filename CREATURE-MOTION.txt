VUC LANG — finite player stop and articulated animal candidate

Player control diagnosis and fix
The previous core had no passive current or buoyancy force. It did retain
exponentially decaying release velocity: ~1.176m normal /1.866m sprint coast,
with visible motion still remaining after half a second. Boundary-clamped
velocity also remained internally nonzero. Source changes use stronger finite
release braking while keeping the same acceleration, cancel blocked velocity,
and roll back unresolved overlaps to the previous clear position. All 388 actual
solid approach/release probes now settle with exactly zero later displacement.

Measured controlled release now settles in 0.289 s / 0.258 m normal and 0.311 s / 0.410 m
sprint. Camera bob follows resolved movement and is exactly absent at rest;
menu auto-pan is removed. Blur, visibility, pointer cancellation and stale
repeat/release cases are covered. Read-only debug exposes input, physical and
resolved velocity, camera position, injuries and rescue count for diagnosis.
Clean Mac baseline idle was already stable; a shared-tab QA driver confounded
some earlier observation. Do not describe that interference as a game defect.

Species mechanics
- Shark: seven weighted joints drive progressive body-to-tail lateral waves,
  caudal propulsion, independent pectoral trim, turn asymmetry and alert load.
  Actual exported motion was not absent: tail travel was about 1.13 m peak-to-peak
  at game scale. Sampled enhanced cruise/faster states reach about 1.60 / 1.97 m.
  There is no separate dorsal joint and the jaw is unweighted; no fake motion
  is claimed for them. Dorsal stabilization follows the weighted trunk.
- Kraken: 58 actual joints (head, mantle and eight seven-joint arms) coordinate
  mantle contraction/recovery, a jet phase peaking at pi/2, delayed traveling
  curls, distal drag, steering and cautious gathering. The initial overlay increased sampled tip motion
  about1.9x; the later explicit unfolding cycle is described below. Mantle width
  contraction exceeds5% in numerical samples.
  There is no independent fin rig, so no fin articulation is invented.
- Jellyfish: four original organisms now exist in the diving world. Nonuniform
  bell mesh contraction drives thrust and recovery; segmented trails retain
  link lengths while lagging and bending against velocity. Individuals have
  different phases, sizes and habitat/player responses. Four bells plus four
  trail meshes use two shared materials, at most 8 transparent draws, 6,876
  pooled vertices and 12,672 high-tier triangles; lower tiers reduce work.
- Warden: the rig has single-bone legs, without knees/ankles. Its moving patrol
  is removed rather than pretending a standing clip is a walk. The near-seabed
  sentinel uses breathing, upper-body/head tracking, jaw/arm warning and delayed
  tail joints. This is not a full foot-contact IK or walking solution.
- Small fish: instanced fusiform geometry now has shader-deformed body/caudal
  motion rather than only rigid object travel. Five schools retain shared
  geometry/material and distance/tier counts.

Integration
Large animals steer with bounded speed/acceleration/turn rate and local
avoidance, using 90 Hz fixed-step kinematics. Cruise, acceleration, turn and
player-response weights transition smoothly. Mantle pulses and body strokes
are tied to the same speed/phase state that moves the actor. This is plausible
animation/kinematic drag, not full fluid, muscle or ecosystem simulation.

Both GLB LODs consume the same phase, clip time and behavior state. Actual
controlled authored tracks are sampled with cached interpolants before joint
offsets: this avoids Three.js skipping unchanged channels and accumulating an
overlay. No GLB bytes were altered. Only the visible variant is posed; phase
continues while hidden. Pause freezes state and pose; reset is deterministic.

Known inherited limits

edge; there is no complete contact IK. The right-mounted torch brightens the
right hand more. The new water remains a procedural opaque approximation,
with its paler overhead color documented in WATER-MAC-QA.md. No new push,
merge or deployment is authorized by this local candidate.
